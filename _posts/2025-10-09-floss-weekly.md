This week I appeared on Hackaday's [FLOSS Weekly podcast](https://hackaday.com/2025/10/08/floss-weekly-episode-850-one-rom-to-rule-them-all/) to talk about my [One ROM](https://onerom.org) open-source project, which aims to be the most flexible ROM replacement for retro systems.

I was interviewed by host Jonathan Bennett and Aaron from [Retro Hack Shack](https://www.youtube.com/c/RetroHackShack) and gave an overview of the project, its goals, some of the technical challenges faced along the way, and what's in store for the future.

If you're new to One ROM but interested in retro computing it's worth checking out!
