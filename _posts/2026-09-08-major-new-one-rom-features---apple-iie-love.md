In this video I finally provide some One ROM love for the Apple IIe, and show off some major new One ROM features including:  
- Header pins controlling external hardware  
- USB logging, including directly from the retro system  
- Control it all from the CLI over USB or from the retro system directly  

Most features require firmware v0.7.2+.  Firmware v0.7.2 should be released within a few days of this video being published.
