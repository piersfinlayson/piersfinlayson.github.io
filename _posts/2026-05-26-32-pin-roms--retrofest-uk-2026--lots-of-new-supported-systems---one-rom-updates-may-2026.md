This video shows the latest systems that One ROM has been tested in and it's not just computers!  The list includes many versions of the Amiga, the Mac SE, Sinclair ZX81, Spectrum and QL, the Robotron from East Germany, MSX and Sega Megadrive cartridges, and also Gottlieb and Williams pinball machines, drum machines and even cars!
