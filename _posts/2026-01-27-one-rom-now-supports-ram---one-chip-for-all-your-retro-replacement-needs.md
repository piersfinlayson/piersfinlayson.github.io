In this video I demonstrate One ROM's new 2K SRAM support on my VIC-20, with a single chip replacing all of the ROM _and_ RAM chips.  I share the moment it finally worked, and then explain how it's done, going into the depths of the RP2350's PIO and DMA's capabilities and explaining some of the lesser known enhancements over the RP2040's PIOs.
