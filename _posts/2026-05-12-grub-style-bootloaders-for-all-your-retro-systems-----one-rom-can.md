In this video I show why you no longer require jumpers or DIP switches to switch between ROM images in your retro system.  With One ROM and its new ROM Bus Contol Protocol implementation, you can switch between ROM images on the fly using applications on the C64.  

The ROM Bus Control Protocol is a new standard for retro systems to communicate with and control ROM emulators, such as One ROM.  GRUB-style bootloaders are available for the Commodore 64 and VIC-20 using One ROM.
