In this video I nearly met my match, laying out a 28 pin version of One ROM Fire using the RP2350 microcontroller.  

I stuck to a 2 layer PCB to keep costs low, and the additional address and CS lines needed, combined with adding a USB-C connector to an already constrained layout, made this an even bigger challenge than the 24 pin version.
