In this video I cover the PCB layout of 4 brand new versions of One ROM - adding an upright USB-C connector the to 24, 28, 32 and 40 pin models.  This is useful when you don't have access to the inline USB-C connector when One ROM is installed.  All of these new designs are based on the latest One ROM versions - including more efficient packing of ROM images and an RGB LED.  

I plan to offer all of these for sale by the end of Septmeber. In the meantime the original upright USB-C 28 pin variant and all of the inline models are available on my online store: <https://shop.piers.rocks>  

Firmware and hardware designs are all open source - see GitHub link below.  The new designs will be published once they've neen validated.
