In this video I let Raspberry Pi get its own back on STM, revealing 5 ways in which the RP2350 microcontroller is better than the STM32F4 - for the most flexible replacement ROM for your retro systems, One ROM.  

The reasons are:  

1. **Dual core** The RP2350 has dual ARM (or RISC-V) cores, in contrast to the STM32F4's single core.  
2. **Overclocking** I've stably overclocked the RP2350 to over 500MHz, but the STM32F4 only to around 250MHz.  
3. **PIOs** The RP2350 parallel IO state machines give me the chance of even more additional processing power.  
4. **Documentation** Both MCUs have good documentation, but the RP2350 wins.  
5. **Marketing** Hardly an engineer's dream reason, but the Raspberry Pi brand gets more publicity for your hobbyist product.
